Why Beleqet

One integration. Every rail your customers already use.

◎

Smart routing, real fallback

ETB traffic routes to EthSwitch first, Telebirr for mobile-first buyers — with Chapa as an automatic fallback if a rail is briefly unavailable.

▦

One ledger, always balanced

Every settlement across every rail lands as a balanced double-entry post. An unbalanced write is refused, never silently accepted.

✦

Signed, verified, idempotent

Every webhook is signature-verified against the raw payload, then double-checked against the rail itself before a single birr moves.

↻

Built for what's next

Foreign-bank Open Banking and Web3/stablecoin settlement ship as first-class rails, ready as licensing and custodianship come online.

Every rail behind one interface.
